Privacy Framework & Corporate Transparency

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At A2Z Recyclers, operated under Gelos Enterprise, we are committed to safeguarding the operational data, profile metrics, and material traffic records shared by our community members and industrial partners. This privacy framework explains how we trace, store, and process your information.

1. Information We Collect

To generate accurate sorting metrics and support live logistics scheduling, we analyze specific data profiles through our secure system nodes:

  • Identity Data: Names, corporate affiliations, and email communication logs handled via the login portal.
  • Logistics Data: Domestic and commercial waste volume history, active tracking records, and scheduled pickup timestamps.
  • Technical Profile Logs: Network timestamps, search queries within our asset library, and session properties.

2. How Data is Utilized

Collected material flows are stripped of direct personal markers wherever possible to maintain security. The records directly assist our field operators in optimizing local waste loops, refining municipal guidelines for communities like Pennant Hills, and compiling statistical insights for environmental audits.

3. Information Storage and Security

All records compiled by our secure nodes are protected using encryption layers preventing unauthorized external traffic access. Data is retained only as long as necessary to comply with Australian industrial standards and Gelos Enterprise accountability bylaws.
