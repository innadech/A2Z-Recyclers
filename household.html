Household Recycling Framework & Sorting Guidelines

Domestic Recycling Guide

Managing residential material streams requires clear sorting methods. This community framework provides explicit classification properties for household trash to minimize processing errors at local collection hubs like Pennant Hills.

Material Classification Matrix

Material Category Examples / Context Target Node
Rigid Plastics Milk bottles, shampoo containers, clean PET flasks Yellow Bin
Paper & Cardboard Unflattened boxes, logistics packaging, newspapers Blue Bin
Residual General Waste Contaminated food wrapping, broken ceramics, soft film Red Bin

Three-Step Domestic Routine

  • 1

    Rinse and Purge

    Ensure all rigid food containers are completely free from biological residues before isolation.

  • 2

    Flatten and Compress

    Crush large cardboard panels and boxes to maximize volume capacity within local drop-off slots.

  • 3

    Segregate Streams

    Never mix soft plastics or plastic bags into the standard automated yellow container loop.
