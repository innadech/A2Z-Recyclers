Recycle Smart for a Greener Future!

Recycling Household Waste

Your home is where you treasure the most valuable things in life. Decluttering unused items is a simple, effective way to reclaim your living space while looking after the planet.

Here are 4 easy recycling techniques to practice at home:

  • Plastic Water Bottles: Avoid letting old plastic bottles pile up. Simply slice them in half to create durable, effective mini planters for starting garden seeds.
  • Aluminum Foil: Instead of tossing clean foil after one use, place it behind shaded garden beds to act as a highly effective natural light reflector.
  • Build an Eco-brick: Pack clean, non-recyclable plastics tightly into empty plastic bottles to create dense, sturdy eco-bricks. These can be used to build modular outdoor structures or garden borders.
  • Repurpose Newspapers: Give your old newspapers a second life by using them as eco-friendly packing wrap for fragile gifts, or combine them with a splash of white vinegar for streak-free window cleaning.
