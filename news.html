Recycling Workshop for Kids

Date Matrix: Friday 16th November to Sunday 18th November

Location: Baden Powell Scout Centre, Pennant Hills, NSW.

This stands as our headline community event for the calendar year. We are honored to host the Australian Minister for the Environment along with key programmatic directors from UNESCO.

The workshop targets participants aged 7 to 18 to establish clean separation workflows early. Note: Children under 16 must be accompanied by an authorized parent or legal guardian throughout the sessions.

To register, visit the Contact Us Form and ensure you include the exact string 'Register me' inside the primary comments area.
