Recycling Business & Organization Waste

Transforming your workspace into a sustainable, eco-friendly environment is easy with a few practical strategy updates. Here is how your business can optimize its waste diversion programs:

  • Introduce Upcycle Stations: Set up a central sorting area to collect and organize functional office gear, like storage units, electronics, and supplies, so your team can reuse them before buying new items.
  • Control Your Supply Stream: Work closely with your vendors to ensure only fully recyclable or compostable materials enter your workplace. Managing what comes in makes sorting and recycling significantly easier.
  • Ditch Single-Use Coffee Cups: Swap out daily takeaway cups for high-quality, reusable company mugs and bottles to instantly cut down your office waste volume.
  • Drive Positive Workplace Habits: Small changes in daily habits—like printing only when necessary, turning off unneeded electronics, and managing climate control efficiently—go a long way in conserving resources.
