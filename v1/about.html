Who We Are & What We Build

Corporate History & Project Lifecycle Systems

Founded as a specialized firm and recently integrated into Gelos corporate infrastructure, A2Z Recyclers has systematically expanded its active recycling programs through structural partnerships with major sustainability initiatives.

Ecogenie Collaboration

A leading Producer Responsibility Organization helping companies process packaging waste at scale. They convert post-consumer PET bottles into sustainable apparel, functional blankets, and retail products.

Good Food Initiative

Co-founded in 2015 to help restaurant networks manage food waste directly at the source. Backed by an analytical mobile tracking application, this initiative has successfully diverted over 9,000 tonnes of material away from landfills.

Green Plastic Network

Connects localized waste pickers to global supply chains, helping premium consumer brands transition to recycled ocean plastic packaging while securing fair market pricing metrics for workers.
